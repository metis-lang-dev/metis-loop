Observed in the real tamarisk river (rddlrepository Tamarisk_MDP_ippc2014, instance 1; 10 episodes x 40 steps; records of sinks/iter0--born--frame-neg-tick.jsonl). Interface (every record): a step starts from a state holding, for EACH observed fluent, either its atom (true) or not_<atom> (false) - tamarisk_at(S) or not_tamarisk_at(S), native_at(S) or not_native_at(S) - for the slots S in s1s1 s1s2 s2s1 s2s2 s3s1 s3s2 s4s1 s4s2; plus the action atoms taken that step (zero or one: eradicate(R) or restore(R), R in r1 r2 r3 r4) and one tick atom; no other atom is given. The next state is read when nothing more can happen, and must again hold, for each observed fluent, exactly one of the atom or not_<atom>; the world's own atoms (empty, cost, unchecked, penalty) are never observed, and the observed next state never depends on them. Every slot changes within ONE step, all at once, each by what held at the START of the step: a plant that arrives this step seeds nothing until the next. The instance is data: slots s1s1 s1s2 lie on reach r1, s2s1 s2s2 on r2, s3s1 s3s2 on r3, s4s1 s4s2 on r4; the river runs r1 -> r2 -> r3 -> r4 (r2 is downstream of r1, r3 of r2, r4 of r3). A slot can hold tamarisk, native, both at once, or neither. The regularities, each a law over every slot and reach alike, every rate in them DATA fitted from the episodes - never a number written in the rules: (1) a slot holding both at the start is a competition: tamarisk stays with one rate and native stays with another, independently of each other, whatever the action (both, either, or neither may remain). (2) A slot holding tamarisk and no native: eradicate on its reach removes the tamarisk at a rate (sometimes it survives); without eradicate on its reach the tamarisk sometimes dies by itself, at a rate. (3) A slot holding native and no tamarisk: restore on its reach keeps the native for sure; otherwise the native sometimes dies by itself, at a rate. (4) An empty slot, for tamarisk: with eradicate on its reach no tamarisk arrives that step; otherwise tamarisk may arrive by itself (at a rate), and EACH tamarisk at the start in the other slot of the same reach, in a slot of the reach just upstream, or in a slot of the reach just downstream, is a separate independent chance of seeding it - the more such sources, the more often it arrives; a source in the same reach, a source upstream (spreading downstream) and a source downstream (spreading upstream, observed rarer) each has its own rate. (5) An empty slot, for native, independently of tamarisk: with restore on its reach a native arrives at a rate (sometimes restore fails); otherwise a native sometimes arrives by itself, at a rate; tamarisk and native can arrive in the same step and then both are held. Each rate is ONE law priced by a weight port and its complement - one rule per law, never one rule per slot, reach or count.
