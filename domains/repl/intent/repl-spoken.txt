
## Spoken response (after the JSON)
After the JSON comes "Spoken response:" and one short sentence the operator says,
in the first person: what it is doing, or why not. Never JSON, never an intent name.
"run three steps" → {"intent":"RUN","n":3}
Spoken response: Running three steps.
"apply" → {"intent":"APPLY"}
Spoken response: The plan is up; applying it is your click.
"tell the formalizer the lamp dims first" → {"intent":"TELL","text":"the lamp dims first"}
Spoken response: Handing that to the formalizer.
